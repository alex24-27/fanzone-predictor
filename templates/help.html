Getting Started Guide & App Tour

Welcome to FanZone Predictor! Review our platform interface tour below to visualize how to submit selections, audit tracking trends, and climb the public ranking systems. Note: If you are on a mobile device, you can access the menu by tapping the hamburger icon (☰) in the top right corner of the screen.

1. Creating Your Profile

Navigate to the top bar and click Register. Create a unique username and a password that meets our dynamic validation checklist. If your username is already taken, an on-screen alert will let you know immediately.

2. Submitting Dashboard Picks

Filter upcoming games instantly by checking your favorite sports leagues. Select your projected winner from the match team block dropdowns and click Save. The button turns blue and displays "Saved" once confirmed. If you modify your pick, the update button reactivates automatically.

2a. Managing Active & In-Progress Picks

The Active Picks dashboard isolates your active predictions in one centralized location so you do not have to dig through the main schedule to find them.

  • Chronological Order: Matches are automatically sorted from earliest start time to latest start time so you can track games happening today first.
  • Modifying Selections: You can quickly adjust and save updates to your picks directly from this page as long as the match has not kicked off.
  • Kickoff Lockout: The moment an event starts, its card dims to indicate that the event has started. The dropdown is disabled, freezing your selection in place.
Active & Locked Selections Feed:

2b. User Pick Allocation Limits (Max 5 Per Team)

To maintain balanced pools and mirror real-world sportsbook liability management, each team in an event can be selected by a maximum of 5 users.

  • Live Tracking Badges: Every match card displays a live counter (e.g., USA: 3/5) showing how many users have chosen that side.
  • Single-Team Lockout: Once a team reaches 5 picks, its dropdown option becomes disabled and its associated counter turns red. New users can only pick the opposing team.
  • Full Match Lockout: If both teams reach 5 picks, a red warning banner appears, preventing any further predictions from users who have not yet bet on the game.
  • Switching Boundaries: If you have already picked a team, you can change your mind and switch sides only if the opposing team has 4 or fewer picks. If they are at 5/5, you are locked into your original selection.
